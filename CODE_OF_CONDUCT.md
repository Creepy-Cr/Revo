# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Revo to be a project where anyone can take part, whatever their background or
experience level. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions and community
channels is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.
- Respect people's time: read the docs and search existing issues before asking.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Anything that would be unacceptable in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who do not
follow this code. If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers
privately through [@RevoLabsHQ](https://x.com/RevoLabsHQ) on X.
